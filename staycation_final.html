Bear Lake

All About Bear Lake:

Bear Lake is on the border of Utah and Idaho. It is nicknamed "Caribbean of the Rockies". Bear Lake is a very popular tourist destination for many people traveling around Utah and Idaho. It's lake it a beautiful turquiose color and is surrounded by diverse wilderness. It spans 20 miles long and 8 miles wide.

  • Scuba Diving

  • Ice Fishing (In the winter)
