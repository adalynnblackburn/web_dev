What is Latin?

According to Britannica, Latin is a Indo-European language. In originally was spoken by small groups located along the Tiber River but eventually spread throughout Italy and most of western and sountern Europe. The modern romance languages developed from Latin. Mainly during the middle ages, Latin was the most widely used language for literary and scholarly purposes.

Some languages that derive from Latin:

  • Portugese
  • Italian
  • Spanish
  • French
  • Romanian

Latin was set up in cases inside of declensions. These cases were nominative, genative, dative, accusative, ablative, and vocative. There was also occasionally a locative case. There was also i-stem declension classes which become completely different, but Latin was a very well structured and organized system with very few exceptions unlike the modern English language.
